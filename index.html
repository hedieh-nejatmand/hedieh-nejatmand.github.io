Hedieh Nejatmand Malari

PhD Student in Psychology · University of New Hampshire

I’m a PhD student in cognitive psychology at the University of New Hampshire. I work with Dr. Casey Roark in the CLEAR Lab, where I design and program behavioral experiments on learning and memory.

My research focuses on working memory and how it shapes learning. I explore this through category learning experiments in which people learn to group unfamiliar images or sounds while keeping other information in mind.

What I’m most passionate about is understanding how the design of everyday tasks shapes what people pay attention to, remember, and learn. I want to use what we know about the limits of attention and memory to reduce unnecessary mental effort and help people learn more efficiently in everyday life.

Outside the lab, I turn little moments from my PhD into short videos. I’m also always up for a trip somewhere new or an evening at the piano.

Research interests: working memory · category learning · attention · metacognition · beliefs and expectations · cognitive performance
